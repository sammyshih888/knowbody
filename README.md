# Knowbody

A 6.1040 personal project about learning more before meeting someone. Two people approve an exchange of selected public profiles. Knowbody gathers source passages and checks explicit links within that selection.

## P2 Alpha (MVP)

[P2 video demo](https://youtu.be/mRnB3gWjQPk)

## User journey

Rachel is planning to meet Simon and wants some context from the pages he chooses to share. Starting with an empty Knowbody database, she registers, signs in, and saves her portfolio and a public club biography as a private card. She creates an invitation and sends the link through their existing chat. Simon registers, signs in, joins the invitation, and offers a card containing two of his own public pages. Each can see their own links and the kinds of pages the other person offers. After Rachel checks that the joining account is Simon's, both approve the current selection. Only then are their links shared and a source brief prepared for each person's pages. Rachel reads excerpts from Simon's pages together, with source links, retrieval times, and page dates when available. The brief also shows any direct link between those selected pages. She can open a source for more context. Rachel then ends the exchange, and both lose shared access inside Knowbody while retaining their own cards and completed briefs.
